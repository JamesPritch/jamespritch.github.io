Rice

Two servings as a side.

Time: 10 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 150g basmati rice, rinsed
  • Some water
  • 2 pinches of salt
  • Some cumin seeds

Preparation

  1. Pour boiled water over the rice to cover twice the height of the rice in the pan. Add salt and cumin seeds to taste.
  2. Bring to the boil and cook for 6-8 minutes.
  3. (Optional) Look yourself hard in the mirror to see if you really needed this recipe.
