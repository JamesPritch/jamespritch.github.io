Spaghetti Carbonara

Serves two.

Time: 15-20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 210g spaghetti
  • 150g peas (optional)
  • 2 eggs
  • 40g grated Parmesan
  • 40g grated Cheddar
  • 80g pack of lardons
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 20g butter
  • A glug of olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Cook spaghetti in boiling water with two pinches of salt and a glug of olive oil for 8-10 minutes.
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs and grated cheeses.
  3. Fry lardons with crushed garlic in butter.
  4. Add the peas to the spaghetti with five minutes to go.
  5. Almost fully drain spaghetti and add it to the pan with lardons and garlic.
  6. Lower the heat, add the egg and cheese mixture, and mix well.
  7. Season with black pepper and salt to taste, and serve.
