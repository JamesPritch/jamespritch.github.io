Spaghetti Carbonara

Time: 30 minutes
Ingredients
  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 large eggs
  • 100g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 150g bacon, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. In a bowl, whisk eggs and grated Parmesan cheese.
  3. Cook bacon in a pan until crispy, then add minced garlic.
  4. Drain spaghetti and add it to the pan with bacon and garlic.
  5. Remove from heat, add the egg and cheese mixture, and toss quickly to coat the spaghetti.
  6. Season with black pepper and salt to taste and serve.
