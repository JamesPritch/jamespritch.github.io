Paella

Two servings.

Time: 30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 125g chorizo, sliced
  • 125g seafood, e.g. fish or prawns
  • 1 red pepper, chopped
  • 1 heaped teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 heaped teaspoon thyme
  • 150g risotto rice
  • 25ml white wine
  • 300 ml chicken stock
  • 30g peas
  • 4 cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • Olive oil, good quality
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Parsley, roughly sliced

Preparation

  1. Fry the chorizo and seafood in the oil and spices, then prepare the pepper and stock.
  2. Add the pepper, peas, tomatoes, and rice and stir thoroughly.
  3. Add the wine and stock and bring to the boil.
  4. Simmer for 10-15 minutes or until the rice has absorbed all the liquid.
  5. Remove from the heat, and serve with parsley.
