Flapjacks à la Purefoy

9 servings. For double that, click here.

Time: 40 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 125g unsalted butter
  • 125g caster sugar
  • 87ml golden syrup
  • 412g porridge oats
  • 60g dried fruit, e.g. dates and raisins, chopped coarsely
  • 40g toasted mixed nuts, chopped coarsely

Preparation

  1. Grease a baking tin measuring 15 x 25 cm, or with similar dimensions.
  2. Add the butter, cut into pieces, with the sugar and syrup. Cook over a moderate heat, stirring until the butter has melted.
  3. Remove from the heat and stir in the oats, chopped fruit, and nuts, until evenly coated in the syrup.
  4. Pour the mixture into the prepared tin and level the surface. Bake at 170°C fan for 25-30 minutes until deep golden around the edges, the mixture will still be very soft in the centre. Leave in the tin until almost cold.
  5. Turn out onto a board and cut into bars.
