Halloumi, Quinoa, and Vegetable Bake

Four servings as a main with a side (peas) too.

Time: 1 hour.

Ingredients

  • 600g peeled sweet potato, cut into 1cm3 cubes
  • 1 medium aubergine, cut into 1cm3 cubes
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 250g packet of cooked quinoa
  • 250g passata or strained tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried mixed herb
  • 250g halloumi, sliced
  • 1½ teaspoon salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 170°C fan.
  2. Put the vegetables on a sheet pan with sides. Drizzle over the olive oil and spinkle over the salt.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven for 35-40 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cooked quinoa, passata/strained tomatoes, and mixed herbs. Top with the sliced halloumi.
  5. Bake for a further 10 minutes and serve.
