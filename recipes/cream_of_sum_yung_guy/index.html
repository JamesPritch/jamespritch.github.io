The Cream of Sum Yung Guy

Recipe is from Fabs in Durham, a favourite in the summer of '24.

Time: 5 minutes.

Ingredients

  • Vodka
  • Amaretto
  • Tia Maria
  • Baileys
  • Cream

Preparation

  1. Mix all ingredients together.
  2. Drink.
